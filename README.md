# Digital Seva & Online Services Shortcuts Portal

A clean, lightning-fast personal dashboard for instant 1-click access to official Indian government portals, banking services, utility bill payments, travel bookings, and educational applications.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **54+ Verified 1-Click Sovereign Shortcuts:**
  - **Government Services:** UIDAI myAadhaar (Update, Appointment, e-Aadhaar Download, PVC Card), Protean / NSDL PAN (New Form 49A, Corrections, e-PAN, Aadhaar Link), ECI Voter ID (Form 6, Form 8, e-EPIC Download), State e-District Certificates (Caste, Income, Domicile), MEA Passport Seva (New, Renewal, Appointments), MoRTH Parivahan Sarathi (Learner's Licence, Driving Licence, Renewal, DL/RC Download), Ration Card NFSA, EPFO PF Services (Unified Portal, UAN Activation, Passbook & Balance, Claim Withdrawal), and Civil Registration (Birth & Death Certificates).
  - **Banking & Financial:** Bharat BillPay (BBPS) Electricity & Water Bills, Domestic Money Transfer, Mobile & DTH Recharge, FASTag Toll Recharge, LIC & Insurance Premium Payments, and Income Tax e-filing.
  - **Travel & Booking:** IRCTC NextGen Train Ticket Booking, Real-Time PNR Status, NTES Live Train Running Status, RedBus / State RTCs, IRCTC Air Flight Booking, and Hotel Reservations.
  - **Education & Recruitment:** SSC / UPSC / IBPS Online Exam Applications, National Career Service (NCS Jobs), Admit Card & Hall Ticket Downloads, Board & National Results (NIC), National Scholarship Portal (NSP), CUET Admissions, APAAR / Academic Bank of Credits, and SBI Collect Online Fee Payments.

- **Personal Pinned Shortcuts (Favorites):**
  - Star any portal to pin it to your personal "My Pinned Shortcuts" shelf at the top of the dashboard.
  - Pinned shortcuts persist in your browser's `localStorage`.

- **Instant Search with 1-Click Launch:**
  - Type to filter instantly or press `Ctrl+K`.
  - Direct "Open" buttons right inside search suggestions.

- **100% Direct Official Sovereign Links:**
  - Every link points directly to authentic `.gov.in`, `.nic.in`, or certified service domains.
  - Zero advertisements, zero third-party proxies, and zero credential storage.

- **Offline Document Checklist Reference:**
  - Clean modal reference showing required documents before applying.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React 18
- **Build Tool:** Vite 5
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (Clean slate/blue personal dashboard theme)
- **Icons:** Lucide React (Tree-shaken, ultra-lightweight)
- **Bundle Size:** ~252 kB (71 kB gzipped)
